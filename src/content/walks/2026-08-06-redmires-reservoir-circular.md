## The day

August 2026. 325m of climbing and 4h52 on the move.

Cloud and sun taking turns, 15 to 16 degrees, wind gusting to 51km/h, dry underfoot all day.

Out with Lizzy, 11:25 to 16:17. 12 photographs from the day, 11:43 to 15:36, 6 of the view, 2 with Ember in them.

## The route

A circuit from the Redmires reservoirs, out along the Long Causeway and north
over the moor towards Low Bradfield, then back round through the plantations.
Dry ground throughout, which is not a given up there.

Worth knowing how the climbing is arranged. Of the 325m, only 44m comes in the
first fifth and 141m waits in the last. The walk out is gentle, and you pay for
it coming home.

The halfway point is a stream crossing on stepping stones, with steps cut into
the far bank. Ember waded rather than used them.

**On the distance.** The GPS says 10.6 miles, but that includes the first mile
or so walked twice: we dropped a piece of kit early on and went back for it. As
a route to follow, this is closer to 8 miles. The GPX download has the whole
thing, retrace and all.

## For the dog

Ember is in 2 of the photographs from this one, both near the end at 14.7km.

She was on a long lead at the stepping stones and went through the water rather
than over them. The stream is the obvious drink on the route, though at 15
degrees it was not that kind of day.

## Parking and practicalities

Redmires Lower reservoir car park, free. No facilities, so sort yourself out
before you set off.
