## The day

August 2026. 325m of climbing and 4h52 on the move.

Cloud and sun taking turns, 15 to 16°C, wind gusting to 32mph, dry underfoot all day.

## Walking with

Joined on this route by Lizzy, and of course Ember.

## The route

Start at the top of Redmires Road, by the Upper reservoir, and pick up the
Long Causeway heading east. It is a
proper old road, a medieval packhorse route and possibly Roman before that,
and it still walks like something built to be walked on.

The ground climbs steadily out to Ringinglow, topping out around 415m at the
five kilometre mark. That is the high point of the day and it comes early.

Then the good bit. The path drops north into Wyming Brook, where the stream
falls through a wooded ravine in a run of cascades between the Redmires and
Rivelin reservoirs. It loses over a hundred metres in a kilometre, on stone
steps, bridges and a set of stepping stones across the water. It is a Wildlife
Trust reserve and part of the Eastern Moors SSSI, and it feels nothing like
the moor you have just crossed.

The low point is 226m in the Rivelin valley, about two thirds of the way round.

**Everything you have descended, you climb back.** Of the day's 325m, only 44m
comes in the first fifth and 141m waits in the last, pulling back out of the
valley to Redmires at 367m. The walk out is gentle and you pay for it coming
home. Worth knowing before you set off.

**On the distance.** The GPS says 10.6 miles, but that includes the first mile
or so walked twice: we dropped a piece of kit early on and went back for it. As
a route to follow this is closer to 8 miles. The GPX download has the whole
thing, retrace and all.

## For your four legged companion

Wyming Brook is the obvious drink on the route and the only reliable water
once you are up on the moor. Worth knowing before a warm day.

The stepping stones across the brook are spaced for boots. Most dogs will
wade instead, which is the sensible choice when you have four feet and no
hands. Ember did.

The descent through the brook is steep and rocky, on cut stone steps. Fine
for a sure-footed dog, but it is not a run-ahead sort of path, and a long
lead is easier than a short one on it.

## Parking and practicalities

**SK 2565 8566**, postcode S10 4QZ. The car park where Redmires Road runs out
and becomes the Long Causeway, alongside the Upper reservoir with the war
memorial opposite. Free and open access. Guidebooks tend to call it Redmires
Plantation car park after the wood behind it.

Worth being specific because there are three Redmires reservoirs and a car
park at each end. This is the western one, a kilometre from the Lower. No
facilities, so sort yourself out before you set off.

The moor section is exposed with no shelter. The Wyming Brook half is the
opposite, wooded and sheltered the whole way down.
