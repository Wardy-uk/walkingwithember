Walking with Ember brings together curated UK walking routes and story-led posts for walkers who want straightforward planning.

At the heart of it, this started as shared hill days with Ember: quiet miles, steady climbs, and the simple reset that comes from being outdoors.

**What this is not.** You will not find turn by turn directions here. No numbered
steps, no "bear left at the third gate". Partly because I would rather be walking
than writing them, and mostly because that is not the point. The point is to show
you a route that works with a dog and let you go and find it yourself.

Every walk comes with the GPX from the day, so you have the line if you want it.
What you do with the ground in between is up to you.

We focus on:

- Accurate distance and difficulty notes
- Parking and access tips
- GPX and map links
- Honest post-walk reflections
- What the route is actually like with a dog: the stiles, the livestock, the water
