## Why this site exists

> “Dog friendly” settles nothing.

Nearly every walking site in the country will tell you a route is dog friendly
and then stop, as though that settles it. It does not tell you there are eleven
stiles and your labrador cannot climb. It does not tell you the last mile is a
verge on a B road, that there is no water for four miles in August, or that the
field at the top has ewes in it every spring.

Those are the things that decide whether a walk is a good day or a long apology
to your dog. So they are written down, on every route, whether or not they make
the walk sound good.

If that sounds obsessive, it is. It is also the entire reason this site exists.
